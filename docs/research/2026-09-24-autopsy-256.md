# Autopsy of 256 games (2026-09-24)

Rules brain, seeds 1000, 5000, 7000 and 9000 (64 each), runs 20260924-152950, -153154, -154203, -154821. Engine as of the shop-stock fix (d84e87160). Tool: `python3 -m pilot.autopsy <runs>`; drill-down: `python3 -m pilot.autopsy --drill <run>/<name> <from> <to>`.

## Headline

**54% of all turns (559k of 1.03M) pass in windows of 300+ turns where depth, XL and the known map do not change.** The pilot is not too weak for where it is so much as it spends half its life stuck, and starves or gets found while stuck. Deaths come at depth about equal to XL (dlvl minus XL at death: 0 in 44, +1 in 40, +2 in 34 of 189 deaths).

## Root causes found by drilling into examples

1. **Two-square oscillation: 132 of 256 games have a run of 50+ steps bouncing between two squares.** Two mechanisms seen:
   - *Explore target flips with position* (B15295009, 1,300 turns, NE/SW/NE/SW): from square A the nearest frontier is X and the first step goes to B; from B the nearest frontier is Y and the first step goes back to A. The target is recomputed every step with no memory of the last one.
   - *Loot and explore alternate* (B15295002, up/down for 900 turns): loot walks one step toward an item, explore walks one step back. Inferred: a hostile is in view from one square and not the other, so the loot gate flips each step. Shops were one instance of this (fixed); it is general.
   - Fix: a sticky target in the default activity (keep the current loot or explore target until reached or unreachable), plus a safety net that blacklists the target when the position has alternated between two squares for 20 steps.
2. **Stale remembered stairs: 12 games pressed `>` 200+ times on a square with no stairs** ("You can't go down here"), one of them 92,426 times to the turn cap, one to starvation. The engine remembers a "staircase down" it once saw there (a mimic, or the Mines and the main dungeon sharing a level number) and never forgets it; the stuck guard passes a turn and the next step presses `>` again. Fix: "You can't go down here" forgets the feature at that square.
3. **Search walls, 184k turns:** the biggest sink, but it is the engine doing what it was told after the real failure (no stairs found) happened. The windows above account for most of it. Fix the loops first, then measure it again.
4. **Deaths to the Watch, guards, shopkeepers and priests: 25 of 189 (13%).** Killer bees 15, wands 14, rothes 10, giant ants 10, hill orcs 9, guards 9, watch captains 6, watchmen 5. The pilot still angers Minetown and vault guards (plan items 5 and 8).

## Proposed order

1. Oscillation: sticky targets and the two-square safety net (touches 132 games).
2. Forget stairs on "You can't go down here" (12 games, one 92k-turn loop).
3. Re-run the autopsy; expect the search-walls and probe totals to fall with the loops gone, then look at what remains.
4. Watch and guard safety (13% of deaths).

Each fix measured as usual: rules pair on 64 seeds, held-out 64, `pilot.compare` margin of error.
