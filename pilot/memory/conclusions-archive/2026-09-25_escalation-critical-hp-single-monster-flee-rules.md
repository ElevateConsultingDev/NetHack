# leaf escalation/critical-hp

At critical or badly hurt HP with a monster adjacent: only fight the threat or flee (step_away/go_to); never eat, quaff, use, check inventory, or pray again if already prayed. Never choose elbereth either, the engrave doesn't finish before the hit lands and is now confirmed lethal even at 15-30% HP (9+ games this batch: hill orc, werejackal x2, rothe x2, kobold lord, giant ant, soldier ant; 25+ cumulative).
