# leaf escalation/critical-hp

At critical or badly hurt HP with a monster adjacent, never eat, quaff/use, check inventory, or rest (incl. rest-on-Elbereth): none stop the next hit and rest does not interrupt on a hit. Below ~25% HP fight the most dangerous adjacent target or flee; don't start Elbereth then wait (7+ this batch, 12+ cumulative).
