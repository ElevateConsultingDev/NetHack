# leaf escalation/badly-hurt

With 2+ hostiles adjacent, target or flee the most dangerous one; don't keep attacking a weaker adjacent monster while a stronger one also lands hits. Don't engrave Elbereth as a stall here either, it does not stop the next hit from landing (5 games this batch).
