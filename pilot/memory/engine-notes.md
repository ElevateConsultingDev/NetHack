# Engine notes (suspected bugs and missing rules; the improvement loop reads this)

Derived from the run records by `pilot.reflect`, alongside `conclusions.md`. Not in the brain's prompt.

- "rest" as a standing order keeps executing turn after turn without a fresh consult even as HP crashes; this batch shows it firing against non-animal monsters too (wererat pack, gold golem, kobold mummy), not just Elbereth-immune animals, so the interrupt-on-hit fix is not type-specific (7+ deaths this batch).
- Elbereth's multi-prompt engrave sequence (select implement, then the "what to write" line) still lets an adjacent monster's attack land and kill mid-sequence; confirmed fatal in at least 3 more games this batch. Needs either a pre-check (skip engrave if HP too low to survive one more hit) or a way to collapse it to one atomic action.
- Cascading same-turn fallback chains (badly hurt -> rest fails -> step_away/go_to fails -> elbereth/pray/fight) still trip the loop detector and end runs as stalls instead of completing one turn's action (multiple games).
- step_away fails with "nowhere further to step" very frequently in corridors/dead ends (9+ games), then the brain retries it or cascades into elbereth; consider having the engine reject a second consecutive step_away against the same target in one turn.
- Critical-HP fallback sometimes issues 'use'/quaff commands when no potion or scroll exists in inventory ("You don't have anything to drink"), wasting the turn (2+ games: gold golem, pony deaths). Engine should validate an item exists before sending the use/quaff order.
- go_to pathfinding fails repeatedly against unreachable coordinates and cascades through several fallback targets in one turn without succeeding or giving up cleanly (multiple games, e.g. small mimic encounters).
- "No way on" stalls persist even after search_walls and search_dead_ends are exhausted; confirmed fix path remains go_to onto the stairs tile followed by a raw '>' keypress.
- go_down can fail "no known way down" even when standing on or next to the actual stairs tile; the map's known-stairs state doesn't update.
- When a consult returns no order or exhausts its fallback chain, the engine defaults to "wait," leaving the pilot idle next to danger or ignoring a Weak/starving state instead of a safer default (recurring, including 3 starvation stalls this batch).
- Loot/pickup/probe routines can loop indefinitely when a peaceful creature blocks the tile or pick_up has no path/target (multiple games).
- explore's/no-way-on's "wait for the monster to move out of the way" fallback never resolves against stationary or slow monsters (molds, fungi, shriekers) and can repeat 10-20+ turns with no re-engagement or fresh consult.
- No safety block on attacking an adjacent gas spore with a thrown weapon (still adjacent = still explodes on you); the floating-eye melee block works correctly as a model to copy.
- Once "keys rejected: walking is the engine's job" fires, the brain keeps retrying nonsensical raw keys for the same stall instead of giving up cleanly to wait.
- Small mimics (difficulty 8) trigger repeated go_to "no path" and step_away failures in at least 2 games this batch; pathfinding around them seems worse than around other blockers, worth a closer look if it recurs.
- History of tried-and-dropped brain-side tweaks (loop iterations 11-27, kept so they aren't retried blind): Hungry-with-no-food go_down-before-clearing; wider rest HP band; multi-round wall search with quality fallback; keep-fighting-instead-of-Elbereth when badly hurt; Excalibur fountain-dipping; eating fresh floating eyes for telepathy; stepping into a corridor/doorway with 2+ hostiles adjacent; sticky explore target with visited-square path cost; remembering every seen-floor square for pathing; sticky explore target plus loot-only-near-hostiles. None produced a net gain; revisit only after the "no way on" or "rest-on-Elbereth doesn't interrupt" problems get a real fix.
