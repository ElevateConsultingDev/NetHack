# Pilot journal

Lessons the brain reads at the start of every game (HaikuBrain puts this file in its prompt). `python3 -m pilot.reflect <run>` rewrites it after a batch; humans edit it too. It is versioned: `git log -p pilot/journal.md` shows how it changed and why.

## Lessons for the brain

- Never fight a shopkeeper, watchman, priest or guard, whatever the difficulty says. Pay (routine keys "p") or leave by the stairs. If a shopkeeper or watch captain is already hostile and using a wand or spell, flee the room/level and break line of sight, not just move toward the stairs; a death ray or bolt still lands while you're in view (B22535006, B07081105 both died to ranged hits while stepping away or heading for stairs one square at a time).
- Elbereth only helps if you then stay on the square and only rest or search. It does not stop @ humans, minotaurs, shopkeepers or guards. (seed: nethackwiki; Astra run 3)
- Badly hurt next to a monster that respects Elbereth: engrave. Next to one that doesn't: step away toward a corridor or take the stairs if they're a few steps away. When cornered (step_away fails with nowhere to go), fight rather than wait, even against a monster tougher than a weak animal: waiting doesn't avoid the attack and burns the turn for nothing (B22535004, jackal; B07081108, giant spider difficulty 7, stalled to the turn limit doing nothing instead of fighting).
- Engraving Elbereth still costs a turn in which adjacent monsters get to attack; don't wait until HP is critical (single digits) to act. Retreat or engrave while HP is still well above a sliver, especially with two or more monsters adjacent (B22443603; this batch B07081103, B07081104, B07081110, B07081115 all died at or near critical HP with multiple monsters adjacent despite calling elbereth or praying just before).
- Floating eye: never melee it (unless blind). Throw daggers at it from a distance; its corpse gives telepathy. If a floating eye blocks the only path forward, throw a weapon to clear it rather than waiting indefinitely; waiting can leave you exposed to something else while stuck (B22535008 waited 1000+ turns and was then hit repeatedly by an unseen monster). (seed: Astra, all three runs)
- Don't search for hidden passages for thousands of turns while hungry or weak: searching and probing burns food and time without progress. If Hungry, eat available food right away instead of continuing to explore or search; waiting until Weak or Fainting to act risks death by starvation or by being caught helpless (B22535001, B22535009, B22535012, B22535015 all starved, fainted, or died fainting this batch).
- Descend about one level per experience level early (Dlvl up to XL+1, XL+2 once armor is decent). Camping on Dlvl 1-2 starves; diving past XL+3 dies (B22535002 died on Dlvl6 at XL4, two levels past the guideline).
- In the Gnomish Mines, dwarves and watchmen hit far harder than their difficulty number suggests (thrown spears, mattocks, wand-fired death rays); retreat toward stairs at first sighting of difficulty 4+ Mines monsters rather than fighting or resting nearby (B07081104 died to a dwarf spear plus giant ant, B07081105 died to a watch captain's death ray, B07081111 was one-shot by a dwarf's mattock).
- Don't rely on repeated prayers in quick succession: a prayer used within roughly 1000 turns of the last one is "not safe" and gives no help; an angered god can drain XP instead of saving you (B22535009 prayed too soon and got "thou art arrogant... you feel foolish," losing a level; B22535011 died right after a "prayer isn't safe" state).
- At a check-in, don't order go_down if the stairs down aren't yet known, and if go_down just failed don't order it again at the next check-in either; switch to explore or search_walls instead. This is still the single most common stall this batch: B07081102 (T2522), B07081106 (T2503), B07081109 (T1520), B07081114 (T2501) all hit "go_down failed: no known way down" and then stalled on wait.

## For the engine (suspected bugs and missing rules; the improvement loop reads this)

- Loot/pickup/probe routines can loop indefinitely when a peaceful creature blocks the tile (a peaceful shopkeeper won't move) or when pick_up has no path to the target square, alternating loot/probe or pick_up/pick_up for many turns without ever completing the action (B22443600, B22443601, B22535003; this batch B07081106 and B07081114 alternate probe/loot at a shop for 8+ turns, and B07081112, B07081113 loop on "pick_up failed: no path to (x, y)" including once while Weak and starving).
- "No way on" with an unkillable or unreachable blocker (a ghost that passes through walls, a floating eye, an unseen engulfing monster) leaves the pilot waiting indefinitely with no fallback tried, running to the turn limit or getting killed while stuck (B22535005, B22535008, B22535010).
- go_to pathfinding can fail repeatedly against the same unreachable coordinate and loop with search_walls or fight without trying an alternate target or giving up cleanly (B22535014; this batch B07081101 cycles go_down/go_to/go_to/fight in one turn, B07081109 cycles go_down/go_to/step_away similarly).
- When a consult returns no order (None), the engine defaults to "wait," leaving the pilot adjacent to a monster and doing nothing turn after turn instead of falling back to a safe default like step_away or fight (B07081100 homunculus, B07081107 jackal, B07081102 housecat, B07081109 coyote, B07081114 grid bug all end their run this way).
- The "What do you want to eat?" prompt (choices field empty) sometimes gets no keystroke response from the brain, stalling the game outright even very early (B22535013, stalled at T219 on a trivial eat prompt).
- A dwarf Valkyrie ate its own race ("You cannibal!  You will regret this!", Luck penalty, aggravate monster) in B22535004. Find which eat path allowed it (floor corpse check, pack food, or a brain order).
- Living on prayer alone fails: B22535009 camped on Dlvl 1 for 10000 turns on 8 prayers about 1000 turns apart; the 9th (gap 1008) was refused ("Thou art arrogant"). The gate is sound; the engine needs food (plan item 2) and a push to descend when food is short (plan item 21).
