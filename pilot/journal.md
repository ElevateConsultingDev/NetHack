# Pilot journal

Lessons the brain reads at the start of every game (HaikuBrain puts this file in its prompt). `python3 -m pilot.reflect <run>` rewrites it after a batch; humans edit it too. It is versioned: `git log -p pilot/journal.md` shows how it changed and why.

## Lessons for the brain

- Never fight a shopkeeper, watchman, priest or guard, whatever the difficulty says. Pay (routine keys "p") or leave by the stairs. If a shopkeeper or watch captain is already hostile and using a wand or spell, flee the room/level and break line of sight, not just move toward the stairs; a death ray or bolt still lands while you're in view (B22535006, B07081105 both died to ranged hits while stepping away or heading for stairs one square at a time).
- Elbereth only helps if you then stay on the square and only rest or search. It does not stop @ humans, minotaurs, shopkeepers or guards. It also does not reliably stop a monster that is already adjacent and mid-attack on the same turn you engrave; several deaths this batch show a hit landing the instant after "rest on Elbereth" from a dwarf or a small mimic (B07324900, B07324902). (seed: nethackwiki; Astra run 3)
- Badly hurt next to a monster that respects Elbereth: engrave. Next to one that doesn't: step away toward a corridor or take the stairs if they're a few steps away. When cornered (step_away fails with nowhere to go), fight rather than wait, even against a monster tougher than a weak animal: waiting doesn't avoid the attack and burns the turn for nothing (B22535004, jackal; B07081108, giant spider difficulty 7, stalled to the turn limit doing nothing instead of fighting).
- Engraving Elbereth still costs a turn in which adjacent monsters get to attack; don't wait until HP is critical (single digits) to act. Retreat or engrave while HP is still well above a sliver, especially with two or more monsters adjacent (B22443603; this batch B07324900, B07324901, B07324902, B07324914, B07324915 all died at or near critical HP right after calling elbereth or praying while monsters were already adjacent).
- Floating eye: never melee it (unless blind). Throw daggers at it from a distance; its corpse gives telepathy. If a floating eye blocks the only path forward, throw a weapon to clear it rather than waiting indefinitely; waiting can leave you exposed to something else while stuck (B22535008 waited 1000+ turns and was then hit repeatedly by an unseen monster). (seed: Astra, all three runs)
- Don't search for hidden passages for thousands of turns while hungry or weak: searching and probing burns food and time without progress. If Hungry, eat available food right away instead of continuing to explore or search; waiting until Weak or Fainting to act risks death by starvation or by being caught helpless. This remains the single most common death overall: this batch alone, 4 of 16 games ended fainted/starving (B07324904 XL5 T6259, B07324909 XL6 T7610, B07324912 XL5 T5220, B07324915 XL3 T2533), all with successful prayers earlier in the game. A prayer that cures Weak/Fainting is only a one-time fix; you still have to eat afterward or you starve again later, sometimes thousands of turns on (B07324904 prayed 3 times over the game but still starved).
- Descend about one level per experience level early (Dlvl up to XL+1, XL+2 once armor is decent). Camping on Dlvl 1-2 starves; diving past XL+3 dies (B22535002 died on Dlvl6 at XL4, two levels past the guideline; B07324904 camped on Dlvl 1-2 for over 6000 turns at XL5 and starved).
- In the Gnomish Mines, dwarves and watchmen hit far harder than their difficulty number suggests (thrown spears, mattocks, wand-fired death rays); retreat toward stairs at first sighting of difficulty 4+ Mines monsters rather than fighting or resting nearby (B07081104 died to a dwarf spear plus giant ant, B07081105 died to a watch captain's death ray, B07081111 was one-shot by a dwarf's mattock; this batch B07324902 and B07324911 died to dwarves in the Mines, B07324908 died to an invisible hill orc, B07324910 died to a gnome's wand of striking, all on or before Dlvl 3-5).
- Don't rely on repeated prayers in quick succession: a prayer used within roughly 1000 turns of the last one is "not safe" and gives no help; an angered god can drain XP instead of saving you. This batch shows the brain re-praying only 7-12 turns after a prior prayer (B07324900, B07324903), far inside the unsafe window; when a "prayer isn't safe" result comes back, don't cycle back to pray again, act immediately with elbereth, step_away, or fight instead (B22535009 prayed too soon and got "thou art arrogant... you feel foolish," losing a level; B22535011 died right after a "prayer isn't safe" state).
- At a check-in, don't order go_down if the stairs down aren't yet known. If explore stalls with no stairs found, order search_walls; if search_walls reports every wall searched twice, follow up with search_dead_ends rather than repeating go_down or go_to blindly. B07324908 used search_dead_ends after search_walls exhausted and avoided a stall; B07324905, B07324906, B07324913, B07324914 all hit "no way on: explored, searched the walls, no stairs down known" and stalled without ever trying search_dead_ends.
- Don't quaff unidentified potions speculatively, especially mid-dungeon with no healing backup; some (this batch, a golden potion) "boil and explode" and can kill outright the instant you drink them (B07324907, died immediately after "Your golden potion boils and explodes!"). Prefer price-ID or a scroll of identify, or only test-quaff at full HP away from danger.

## For the engine (suspected bugs and missing rules; the improvement loop reads this)

- Loot/pickup/probe routines can loop indefinitely when a peaceful creature blocks the tile (a peaceful shopkeeper won't move) or when pick_up has no path to the target square, alternating loot/probe or pick_up/pick_up for many turns without ever completing the action (B22443600, B22443601, B22535003; earlier batch B07081106, B07081112, B07081113 also showed this).
- "No way on" stalls are broader than just an unkillable blocker: this batch, four games (B07324905, B07324906, B07324913, B07324914) hit "explored, searched the walls, no stairs down known" and stalled with no hostile monster involved at all, just an apparently exhausted map. The engine has no fallback beyond wait once search_walls is exhausted and search_dead_ends isn't automatically tried (earlier evidence for the blocker case: B22535005, B22535008, B22535010).
- go_to pathfinding can fail repeatedly against the same unreachable coordinate and cascade through pick_up/go_to/go_to/explore in a single turn without trying an alternate target or giving up cleanly (B22535014; earlier B07081101, B07081109; this batch B07324906 chains pick_up->go_to->go_to->explore against unreachable coordinates and then stalls).
- When a consult returns no order (None), the engine defaults to "wait," leaving the pilot adjacent to a monster and doing nothing turn after turn instead of falling back to a safe default like step_away or fight (B07081100 homunculus, B07081107 jackal, B07081102 housecat, B07081109 coyote, B07081114 grid bug all end their run this way).
- Cascading same-turn fallback chains (critical HP -> step_away fails -> go_to fails -> elbereth -> "prayer isn't safe" -> fight) can trip the engine's loop detector and end the run as a "brain loop" stall instead of completing one turn's action, even though each individual order made sense given the prior failure (B07324903).
- The "What do you want to eat?" prompt (choices field empty) sometimes gets no keystroke response from the brain, stalling the game outright even very early (B22535013, stalled at T219 on a trivial eat prompt).
- A dwarf Valkyrie ate its own race ("You cannibal!  You will regret this!", Luck penalty, aggravate monster) in B22535004. Find which eat path allowed it (floor corpse check, pack food, or a brain order).
- Living on prayer alone fails: B22535009 camped on Dlvl 1 for 10000 turns on 8 prayers about 1000 turns apart; the 9th (gap 1008) was refused ("Thou art arrogant"). This batch's B07324904 shows the same shape at a slower pace: 3 successful prayers over 6000+ turns still ended in starvation death, because the underlying food shortage was never addressed. The gate is sound; the engine needs food (plan item 2) and a push to descend or forage when food is short (plan item 21).
