# Pilot journal

Lessons the brain reads at the start of every game (HaikuBrain puts this file in its prompt). `python3 -m pilot.reflect <run>` rewrites it after a batch; humans edit it too. It is versioned: `git log -p pilot/journal.md` shows how it changed and why.

## Lessons for the brain

- Never fight a shopkeeper, watchman, priest or guard, whatever the difficulty says. Pay (routine keys "p") or leave by the stairs. If a shopkeeper, watch captain, or any wand/spell-wielding monster is already hostile, flee the room/level and break line of sight, not just move toward the stairs or a go_to target one square at a time; a death ray, bolt, or wand strike still lands while you're in view (B22535006, B07081105 both died to ranged hits while stepping away; B09015405 died to a hobgoblin's wand the same way; a mind flayer's wand of striking killed B09015400 at critical HP while it was stepping away then heading to a go_to target instead of breaking line of sight). A hobgoblin's wand can do the same to a non-guard monster: stepping away one square at a time still let two more zaps land and kill (B08325405).
- Elbereth only helps if you then stay on the square and only rest or search. It does not stop @ humans (including a wererat back in human form), minotaurs, shopkeepers, guards, or mindless monsters like gray oozes and golems, which ignore it entirely and keep biting/hitting right through an engrave (B07324900, B07324902 dwarf/mimic; B07432505 and B07432512 gray ooze; B07432512 paper golem; B08204608 gold golem; B09015415 wererat bit through two elbereth engravings before the character died). Ordinary animals can also land hits through engrave+rest: giant ants, a giant bat, a gecko, a rock mole (B08070102/104/108/110; B08204607). Treat engrave+rest as a gamble, not a guaranteed safe harbor: if you take a hit after one turn resting on Elbereth, stop resting and flee or fight instead of repeating rest blindly.
- Step_away does not guarantee safety either: a monster already adjacent still gets its attack before you clear distance, and reissuing step_away with a bigger max_steps is not a fix if the monster is at least as fast as you (B09015401: a giant ant kept biting through two step_away orders, at max_steps 5 then 10, and killed the character; B09015404: an iguana kept landing hits through an ongoing fight with no escape working). If step_away fails once against an adjacent fast attacker, switch to fight or elbereth rather than repeating it or escalating step count.
- Badly hurt next to a monster that respects Elbereth: engrave. Next to one that doesn't (oozes, golems, shopkeepers, rock moles, human-form lycanthropes, etc.): step away toward a corridor or take the stairs if close. When cornered (step_away fails with nowhere to go), fight rather than engrave-and-rest or wait: two deaths (B08204607 rock mole, B08204608 gold golem) came from choosing elbereth+rest after step_away failed, and the monster kept hitting through the rest anyway. EXCEPT a floating eye: never melee a floating eye even when cornered with nothing to throw, since paralysis on a hit is worse than losing a turn waiting for another option (B07432509).
- Don't order "rest" as your badly-hurt response while a hostile monster is still adjacent or in view: rest fails immediately with "something's in view," wastes the decision, and often cascades into a brain-loop stall instead of a real escape (B07432501 garter snake, B07432507 orc zombie; B08070106 hobgoblin, where rest was reissued repeatedly while being hit and the character died fainted, mid-combat). Only rest once the monster has fled, died, or line of sight is broken.
- Engraving Elbereth still costs a turn in which adjacent monsters get to attack, and the critical-HP prayer/elbereth/fight fallback does not reliably save you, whichever specific order it lands on: across recent batches most deaths came right after that exact chain (8 of 11 in one batch, 6 of 7 in the next, 7 of 9 after that, and 9 of 11 in the most recent batch: B09015400, B09015402, B09015405, B09015407, B09015408, B09015409, B09015411, B09015412, B09015415). One earlier game also shows the engrave itself can fail outright ("You can't reach the ground") before Elbereth is ever written, so the turn is lost to a fatal hit anyway (B08204604). Retreat or engrave while HP is still well above a sliver, especially with two or more monsters adjacent or if step_away has already failed, and treat the fallback as a last resort, not a plan.
- At critical HP with a monster adjacent, don't spend the turn opening inventory ('i'), eating, quaffing an unidentified potion, or issuing a non-combat order like go_down or a malformed use order: none of these stop the enemy's next hit and all burn the one decision you have to elbereth, flee, or fight. Examples: B07432511 sewer rat after an inventory-check order; B08070111 quaff order with three monsters adjacent; B08204602 died to a rothe right after an "eat" order at critical HP; B08204611/B08204615 died to a rope golem after an unidentified-potion quaff triggered a "Call a ___ potion" prompt that burned turns; B08325404 died the same turn a "critical HP, prayer isn't safe" fallback chose go_down instead of fight or elbereth; B08325412 died after the same fallback chose eat, then fight, one turn too late; B09015411 died after the fallback chose "use" with an item letter that wasn't a real potion, producing no effect before the fatal bite. When prayer isn't safe, go straight to fight or elbereth, not a non-defensive or malformed order.
- Floating eye: never melee it (unless blind), even when cornered. Throw daggers at it from a distance; its corpse gives telepathy. If a floating eye blocks the only path forward and you have nothing to throw, waiting is safer than fighting it, even if the wait runs to thousands of turns (B22535008 waited 1000+ turns; B08204610 and B08204614 waited out a blocking floating eye; B08325414 and B09015414 both waited thousands of turns with a floating eye blocking the only way on and never took a fatal hit from it; B09015410 waited out a floating eye the same way).
- Don't search for hidden passages for thousands of turns while hungry or weak: searching and probing burns food and time without progress. If Hungry, eat available food right away instead of continuing to explore or search; waiting until Weak or Fainting to act risks death or a stuck run. This remains the single most common failure mode overall: earlier batches stalled Weak/no-safe-food (B07432508, B07432514, B08204601) or died fighting while Weak (B07432510, B08070114), and B09015402 shows even a character with two well-spaced successful prayers still died fainted from starvation to a rothe once the food ran out again. A prayer that cures Weak/Fainting is only a one-time fix; you still have to eat afterward or the same problem returns.
- Descend about one level per experience level early (Dlvl up to XL+1, XL+2 once armor is decent). Camping starves; diving too far too fast dies (B22535002 died on Dlvl6 at XL4, two levels past the guideline; B07324904 camped on Dlvl 1-2 for over 6000 turns at XL5 and starved).
- In the Gnomish Mines, dwarves, watchmen, and giant spiders hit far harder than their difficulty number suggests (thrown spears, mattocks, wand-fired death rays, poisoned bites); retreat toward stairs at first sighting of difficulty 4+ Mines monsters rather than fighting or resting nearby (B07081104, B07081105, B07081111; B07324902, B07324911; B08070107, B08070108; B09015407 and B09015408 both died to giant spiders' poisoned bites after go_to/step_away failed to open a retreat route). A string of many smaller Mines monsters back-to-back (rock mole, rothe, cave spider, fox) can also grind HP down through repeated step_away/fight trades even though none of them is individually deadly; retreat fully to a corridor or the stairs instead of trading blows with each new arrival (B08325408 died to a fox at the end of just such a sequence).
- Don't rely on repeated prayers in quick succession: a prayer used within roughly 1000 turns of the last one is "not safe" and gives no help; an angered god can drain XP instead of saving you (B22535009 lost a level after praying too soon; B22535011 died right after a "prayer isn't safe" state; the food-stall games B07432508 and B07432514 show the same shape). Spaced well apart, prayer does keep working repeatedly: B08204614 got seven successful prayers roughly 1000+ turns apart over an 8700-turn game, B08325406/B08325414 and B09015414 (seven prayers ~1000 turns apart over 8700 turns) confirm the same, so pacing matters more than avoiding prayer altogether; but B09015402 shows spacing prayers correctly still doesn't solve an underlying food shortage, it only buys time.
- At a check-in, don't order go_down if the stairs down aren't yet known. If explore stalls with no stairs found, order search_walls; if search_walls reports every wall searched twice, follow up with search_dead_ends. This is the single most common stall shape: one batch alone saw 8 of 16 games end in a "no way on" stall or loop even after both search routines ran out, and later batches keep showing the same shape (B08325406, B08325411, B08325413, B08325414; B09015403, B09015406, B09015410, B09015413, B09015414). There is still no reliable brain-side fix once both are exhausted; expect some runs to stall this way, and don't keep repeating go_down or go_to blindly when they do.
- When go_to fails with "no path to (x,y)", don't fall back to raw movement keys like 'k' or '<': the engine rejects those ("keys rejected: walking is the engine's job" or "You can't go up here") and wastes the turn/consult. Order explore or pick a different go_to target instead (B08070100; B08204607 issued "keys" three times in a row as a badly-hurt response with no effect before falling through to elbereth; B09015408 issued "keys": "<" twice in a row at critical HP with no upstairs present, wasting the turn before the giant spider's bite killed the character).
- Don't quaff unidentified potions speculatively, especially mid-dungeon with no healing backup; some "boil and explode" and can kill outright the instant you drink them (B07324907). Quaffing at critical HP is worse even if the potion itself doesn't kill you: the follow-up "Call a ___ potion" naming prompt takes extra turns to answer while an adjacent monster keeps attacking, which killed two characters in one batch to the same rope golem shape (B08204611, B08204615). Prefer price-ID or a scroll of identify, or only test-quaff at full HP away from danger.

## For the engine (suspected bugs and missing rules; the improvement loop reads this)

- Loot/pickup/probe routines can loop indefinitely when a peaceful creature blocks the tile or when pick_up has no path/target, alternating loot/probe or pick_up/pick_up for many turns without completing (B22443600, B22443601, B22535003, B07081106, B07081112, B07081113; B07432514; B08204610). Loot and "walk to the fresh kill" can also alternate for 50+ turns inside a shop until the corpse rots.
- go_to pathfinding fails repeatedly against unreachable coordinates and cascades through fallback orders in a single turn without trying an alternate target or giving up cleanly; this remains a dominant failure shape (B08070100, B08070113; B08204606, B08204600; B09015407 go_to failed then immediately fell through to fight against a giant spider).
- "No way on" stalls persist even after search_walls and search_dead_ends are both tried, or before search_dead_ends is even attempted: the engine has no further fallback beyond wait once both are exhausted (B07432513, B07432515; B08070112/113/115; B08204600/603/609/610/612/613/614; B08325406, B08325411, B08325413, B08325414; B09015403, B09015406, B09015410, B09015413, B09015414). B08204609 shows a locked door kicked open and re-closed/re-locked across two cycles before the run gave up on that route.
- The "What are you looking for? The exit?" search prompt appears repeatedly (10+ turns straight in B07432515 and B08070112, B08070115); this looks like a vault/hidden-area search state the engine doesn't resolve or route around, burning many turns or contributing to a stall.
- When a consult returns no order (None) or exhausts its fallback chain, the engine defaults to "wait," leaving the pilot doing nothing next to danger or a genuine dead end instead of a safer default (B07081100/102/107/109/114; B07432500/508/513/514; B08070100/112/113/114/115; B08204600/601/603/609/610/612/613/614; B08325406, B08325410, B08325411, B08325413, B08325414; B09015403, B09015406, B09015410, B09015413, B09015414 all end the same way).
- Cascading same-turn fallback chains (badly hurt -> rest fails "something's in view" -> step_away/go_to fails -> elbereth/pray/fight) can trip the engine's loop detector and end the run as a "brain loop" stall instead of completing one turn's action, even though each individual order made sense given the prior failure (B07324903; B07432501, B07432507, B07432509; B08204606; B08325400, where step_away/pray/go_to/elbereth/fight were all issued in the same turn before the run stalled).
- The engine has no safety block on a "fight" order targeting a floating eye, even though meleeing one is a well-known near-guaranteed paralysis risk; consider refusing or flagging that specific order (B07432509).
- Eat-related prompts (empty-choices getlin/yn for "What do you want to eat?") mostly get answered via keys without stalling now (B08204612 handled cleanly), but a prior hard stall (B22535013 at T219) and an unresolved case where an "e" order changed nothing mid-guard-encounter (iteration 6 game 07, T4308) show the underlying handling isn't fully robust.
- A dwarf Valkyrie ate its own race ("You cannibal!") in B22535004; the eat path that allowed this (floor corpse check, pack food, or brain order) is still unidentified.
- Living on prayer alone fails: B22535009 camped on Dlvl 1 for 10000 turns on 8 prayers before a 9th was refused. Smaller-scale versions keep recurring (B07432508, B07432514; B08070106, B08070114; B08204601, B08204605; B08325410): the gate is sound, but the engine still has no push to find/carry more food or descend when food runs short, even when prayers are correctly spaced (B09015402 got two "ok" prayers 1030 turns apart and still died fainted).
- Multi-prompt action sequences leave a window where an adjacent monster's attack can land and kill the character before the sequence completes, even though the order was issued in time: engraving Elbereth's "choose implement" then "type text" prompts (B08070103 Elvenking, B08070105 gnome lord; B08325415 and B09015412, where a hostile monster's hit landed between the two engrave prompts and killed the character before Elbereth was written), and quaffing an unidentified potion then answering its "Call a ___ potion" naming prompt (B08204611, B08204615). B08204604 shows a related failure where the engrave itself fails outright with "You can't reach the ground," denying Elbereth entirely regardless of timing. Similarly, a "use" order with a malformed item reference (q command paired with item letter 'i', not an actual potion) at critical HP produced no visible effect and wasted the last turn before death (B09015411).
- A failed pick_up (no path to target) can cascade the same-turn fallback chain straight into an "elbereth" order even against a trivial first-sight monster with no real threat, wasting a turn on an unnecessary engrave (B08204610: pick_up failed against a kobold zombie 1 away -> elbereth).
- explore's fallback "wait for the monster to move out of the way" can repeat for several turns while an adjacent monster keeps landing hits, with no badly-hurt consult firing until the character is dead (B08325401: after go_down/go_to failed near a hobgoblin in the Mines, the pilot issued "wait for the hobgoblin to move out of the way" repeatedly and died with no intervening consult).
- Standing orders (fight, rest) still appear to override or run underneath an explicitly chosen escape/elbereth order in the same turn window: B08325403's step_away and pray orders against a dwarf were followed by "You hit the dwarf"/"You miss the dwarf" messages consistent with a standing fight order still running; B09015415 shows the same shape more directly, with two consecutive "elbereth" orders each followed by a logged "standing order: fight the sewer rat" / "standing order: fight the grid bug" action instead, and the wererat killed the character mid-fight. Two brain-side workarounds were tried and dropped in earlier loop iterations without fixing this: (3) stop standing orders pre-empting elbereth/step_away/rest/pray routines, and (4) have r_elbereth give up when attacked or losing HP while resting on the square, both washes or regressions on the deterministic rules-brain pair (melee deaths 3->6 then 3->3, avg deepest 3.31->2.94->3.06). A separate tried-and-dropped fix (loop iteration 9: Weak-with-no-food -> go_down, plus NetHack travel capped at 40 steps/level for remembered-stairs-without-a-path) also didn't reliably help (hunger endings 5->5, avg deepest 4.00->4.00; travel bounced for 10000 turns when uncapped). The core issue, an escape/elbereth order not actually taking effect because a standing order fires instead, still needs a real engine-side fix rather than a brain-side rule change.
