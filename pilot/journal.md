# Pilot journal

Lessons the brain reads at the start of every game (HaikuBrain puts this file in its prompt). `python3 -m pilot.reflect <run>` rewrites it after a batch; humans edit it too. It is versioned: `git log -p pilot/journal.md` shows how it changed and why.

## Lessons for the brain

- Never fight a shopkeeper, watchman, priest or guard, whatever the difficulty says. Pay (routine keys "p") or leave by the stairs. If a shopkeeper or watch captain is already hostile and using a wand or spell, flee the room/level and break line of sight, not just move toward the stairs; a death ray or bolt still lands while you're in view (B22535006, B07081105 both died to ranged hits while stepping away or heading for stairs one square at a time).
- Elbereth only helps if you then stay on the square and only rest or search. It does not stop @ humans, minotaurs, shopkeepers, guards, or mindless monsters like gray oozes and golems, which ignore it entirely and keep biting/hitting right through an engrave (B07324900, B07324902 dwarf/mimic; B07432505 and B07432512 gray ooze; B07432512 paper golem). This batch, ordinary animals kept attacking through engrave+rest too: giant ants (B08070102, B08070108), a giant bat (B08070104), and even a gecko (B08070110) all landed hits after the character engraved and settled in to rest. Treat engrave+rest as a gamble, not a guaranteed safe harbor: if you take a hit after one turn resting on Elbereth, stop resting and flee or fight instead of repeating rest blindly.
- Badly hurt next to a monster that respects Elbereth: engrave. Next to one that doesn't (oozes, golems, shopkeepers, etc.): step away toward a corridor or take the stairs if close. When cornered (step_away fails with nowhere to go), fight rather than wait, even against a monster tougher than a weak animal, EXCEPT a floating eye: never melee a floating eye even when cornered with nothing to throw, since paralysis on a hit is worse than losing a turn waiting for another option (B07432509, step_away and throw both failed and the fallback chain ended in "fight the floating eye").
- Don't order "rest" as your badly-hurt response while a hostile monster is still adjacent or in view: rest fails immediately with "something's in view," wastes the decision, and often cascades into a brain-loop stall instead of a real escape (B07432501 garter snake, B07432507 orc zombie; B08070106 hobgoblin, where rest was reissued repeatedly while being hit and the character died fainted, mid-combat). Only rest once the monster has fled, died, or line of sight is broken.
- Engraving Elbereth still costs a turn in which adjacent monsters get to attack, and the critical-HP prayer/elbereth/fight fallback does not reliably save you: this batch, 8 of 11 deaths came right after that exact chain (B08070102, B08070103, B08070104, B08070105, B08070108, B08070109, B08070110, B08070111). Two of those (B08070103 Elvenking, B08070105 gnome lord) died between choosing the writing implement and actually typing "Elbereth", so the engraving was never even placed. Retreat or engrave while HP is still well above a sliver, especially with two or more monsters adjacent, and treat the fallback as a last resort, not a plan.
- At critical HP with a monster adjacent, don't spend the turn opening inventory ('i') or quaffing an unidentified potion: neither stops the enemy's next hit and both burn the one decision you have to elbereth, flee, or fight (B07432511 died to a sewer rat after an inventory-check order; B08070111 died after a critical-HP "use"/quaff order with three monsters adjacent).
- Floating eye: never melee it (unless blind), even when cornered. Throw daggers at it from a distance; its corpse gives telepathy. If a floating eye blocks the only path forward and you have nothing to throw, waiting is safer than fighting it (B22535008 waited 1000+ turns and was then hit by something else, which is bad, but still better than the guaranteed paralysis risk of meleeing it). (seed: Astra, all three runs)
- Don't search for hidden passages for thousands of turns while hungry or weak: searching and probing burns food and time without progress. If Hungry, eat available food right away instead of continuing to explore or search; waiting until Weak or Fainting to act risks death or a stuck run. This remains the single most common failure mode overall: earlier batches stalled Weak/no-safe-food (B07432508, B07432514) or died fighting while Weak (B07432510), and B08070114 shows the same shape again: Weak then Fainting stall at T4040 after a second prayer failed, with the brain repeatedly pressing '<' to flee upstairs instead of finding food. A prayer that cures Weak/Fainting is only a one-time fix; you still have to eat afterward or the same problem returns.
- Descend about one level per experience level early (Dlvl up to XL+1, XL+2 once armor is decent). Camping starves; diving too far too fast dies (B22535002 died on Dlvl6 at XL4, two levels past the guideline; B07324904 camped on Dlvl 1-2 for over 6000 turns at XL5 and starved).
- In the Gnomish Mines, dwarves and watchmen hit far harder than their difficulty number suggests (thrown spears, mattocks, wand-fired death rays); retreat toward stairs at first sighting of difficulty 4+ Mines monsters rather than fighting or resting nearby (B07081104, B07081105, B07081111; B07324902, B07324911 died to dwarves in the Mines; this batch B08070107 died to a giant spider, difficulty 7, on Mines Dlvl7, and B08070108 died to a giant ant/gray ooze combo on Mines Dlvl5, both after lingering instead of retreating).
- Don't rely on repeated prayers in quick succession: a prayer used within roughly 1000 turns of the last one is "not safe" and gives no help; an angered god can drain XP instead of saving you (B22535009 lost a level after praying too soon; B22535011 died right after a "prayer isn't safe" state; the food-stall games B07432508 and B07432514 show the same shape; this batch's B08070114 shows it again, a second prayer at T3266 was refused, and the run stalled Weak/Fainting afterward instead of finding food).
- At a check-in, don't order go_down if the stairs down aren't yet known. If explore stalls with no stairs found, order search_walls; if search_walls reports every wall searched twice, follow up with search_dead_ends. Even that isn't a guaranteed fix: this batch three more games stalled the same way, two after search_dead_ends was exhausted (B08070112, B08070113) and one that never got that far before the brain gave up (B08070115), all ending in the "What are you looking for? The exit?" prompt loop and a wait stall. If both search_walls and search_dead_ends are exhausted, order explore toward the darkest remaining unexplored area rather than repeating go_down or go_to blindly; there is currently no known brain-side fix once that fails, so expect some of these to remain stalls.
- When go_to fails with "no path to (x,y)", don't fall back to raw movement keys like 'k': the engine rejects those ("keys rejected: walking is the engine's job") and wastes the turn/consult. Order explore or pick a different go_to target instead (B08070100).
- Don't quaff unidentified potions speculatively, especially mid-dungeon with no healing backup; some "boil and explode" and can kill outright the instant you drink them (B07324907, died immediately after "Your golden potion boils and explodes!"). Prefer price-ID or a scroll of identify, or only test-quaff at full HP away from danger.

## For the engine (suspected bugs and missing rules; the improvement loop reads this)

- Loot/pickup/probe routines can loop indefinitely when a peaceful creature blocks the tile or when pick_up has no path/target, alternating loot/probe or pick_up/pick_up for many turns without completing (B22443600, B22443601, B22535003, B07081106, B07081112, B07081113; B07432514 repeated "probe the dark" and "There is nothing here to pick up" for many turns straight into a starvation stall).
- go_to pathfinding fails repeatedly against unreachable coordinates and cascades through fallback orders in a single turn without trying an alternate target or giving up cleanly; this was the dominant failure shape across recent batches, including this one (B08070100 no path to (56,12)/(56,10), B08070113 no path to (28,14)/(40,5)), several of which end in a death or stall right after the failed go_to.
- "No way on" stalls persist even after search_walls and search_dead_ends are both tried, or before search_dead_ends is even attempted: the engine has no further fallback beyond wait once both are exhausted, and no automatic retry of unexplored dark tiles (B07432513, B07432515; this batch B08070112, B08070113, B08070115).
- The "What are you looking for? The exit?" search prompt appears repeatedly (10+ turns straight in B07432515 and B08070112, B08070115 this batch); this looks like a vault/hidden-area search state the engine doesn't resolve or route around, burning many turns or contributing to a stall.
- When a consult returns no order (None) or exhausts its fallback chain, the engine defaults to "wait," leaving the pilot doing nothing next to danger or a genuine dead end instead of a safer default (B07081100, B07081102, B07081107, B07081109, B07081114; B07432500, B07432508, B07432513, B07432514; this batch B08070100, B08070112, B08070113, B08070114, B08070115 all end the same way).
- Cascading same-turn fallback chains (badly hurt -> rest fails "something's in view" -> step_away/go_to fails -> elbereth/pray/fight) can trip the engine's loop detector and end the run as a "brain loop" stall instead of completing one turn's action, even though each individual order made sense given the prior failure (B07324903; B07432501, B07432507, B07432509).
- The engine has no safety block on a "fight" order targeting a floating eye, even though meleeing one is a well-known near-guaranteed paralysis risk; consider refusing or flagging that specific order (B07432509).
- The "What do you want to eat?" prompt (choices field empty) has previously stalled a game outright (B22535013 at T219); it keeps getting answered via keys without stalling (B07432515-era batch, and this batch's B08070111, B08070112 "Eat it?" prompt too), but the underlying empty-choices prompt still needs a robust default in case it recurs uncontrolled.
- A dwarf Valkyrie ate its own race ("You cannibal!") in B22535004; the eat path that allowed this (floor corpse check, pack food, or brain order) is still unidentified.
- Living on prayer alone fails: B22535009 camped on Dlvl 1 for 10000 turns on 8 prayers before a 9th was refused. Smaller-scale versions keep recurring (B07432508, B07432514; this batch B08070106 four prayers then starved to death fighting a hobgoblin, B08070114 two prayers then a Weak/Fainting stall): the gate is sound, but the engine still has no push to find/carry more food or descend when food runs short.
- The "keys" order gets rejected when used for raw movement (e.g., pressing 'k' to walk) after go_to fails to find a path; the engine has no fallback here beyond an implicit wait, leaving the brain stuck after exhausting go_down/go_to/search_dead_ends (B08070100).
- Engraving Elbereth is a two-prompt sequence (choose writing implement, then type the text); an adjacent monster's attack can land between the two prompts and kill the character before Elbereth is ever written, even though the order was issued in time (B08070103 Elvenking, B08070105 gnome lord).
